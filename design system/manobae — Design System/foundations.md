\# Foundations

\#\# North star  
Digital scrapbook × modern editorial design × playful private relationship space.

\#\# Brand adjectives  
Warm, intimate, curious, handmade, calm, witty, nostalgic, alive.

\#\# Avoid  
Corporate SaaS, childish Valentine's graphics, neon gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, identical rounded cards, giant dashboard headings, random emojis, excessive hearts, and decorative clutter.

\#\# Experience principles  
1\. Content is the decoration. Photos, handwriting, dates, places, and tiny observations lead.  
2\. One screen, one emotional focus. Secondary actions stay quiet.  
3\. Earn delight. Strong effects appear on reveals, anniversaries, completed dreams, or identity entry—not every click.  
4\. Imperfection is controlled. Rotation stays between \-1.2° and 1.2°; only scrapbook objects rotate.  
5\. The product grows visibly. Empty states invite the first meaningful contribution.  
6\. Private actions explain when content reveals and to whom.  
7\. Shared objects visually combine both identities.

\#\# Layout  
\- Mobile: single narrative column; 16px gutters.  
\- Tablet: 24px gutters; optional two-column compositions.  
\- Desktop: centered content, max width 1180px; reading width 680px.  
\- Bottom navigation on mobile; compact top navigation on desktop.  
\- Use asymmetry in photo/memory compositions, not in forms or data entry.

\#\# Surface language  
\- Page: warm paper.  
\- Standard UI: calm flat surface with thin ink border.  
\- Memory: paper note or photo.  
\- Dream: postcard or ticket.  
\- Place: saved receipt/map label.  
\- Question: folded note.  
\- Milestone: framed keepsake.

\#\# Radius family  
\- control: 10px  
\- surface: 16px  
\- soft surface: 22px  
\- paper/photo: 4px  
\- ticket: 12px  
\- pill: 999px  
Identity themes may shift radius by no more than 2px. Never globally apply rounded-xl.

\#\# Borders and shadows  
Borders do more work than shadows. Use 1px neutral ink at 10–18% opacity. Shadows:  
\- lift-1: 0 2px 10px rgba(45,35,37,.07)  
\- lift-2: 0 10px 28px rgba(45,35,37,.10)  
\- photo: 0 8px 24px rgba(35,28,30,.14)  
No black drop shadows or glowing UI.

\#\# Accessibility  
Minimum body 16px, minimum touch target 44×44px, visible focus ring, status never communicated by color alone, alt text for meaningful photos, empty alt for decorative SVGs, and reduced-motion parity.
