\# manobae — Design System

Status: prefilled source of truth for the private two-person relationship website.

\#\# Product feeling  
A private digital scrapbook that is warm, playful, romantic, tactile, and slightly imperfect. It must feel collected over time—not generated from a dashboard template.

\#\# Non-negotiables  
\- Mobile-first; excellent from 360px upward.  
\- Persian is first-class RTL, not a translated LTR layout.  
\- English and Persian may coexist in one memory.  
\- Themes are token palettes and personality accents, not separate products.  
\- Shared content uses the shared theme regardless of author.  
\- Events are the visual/data center for calendar, memories, places, and media.  
\- Prefer one meaningful composition over a grid of generic cards.  
\- Decorative elements support content; they never cover text or controls.  
\- Accessibility, readable contrast, reduced motion, and 44px touch targets are mandatory.

\#\# Product loops  
\- Daily: question, mood, appreciation.  
\- Weekly: decisions, date roulette, plans, saved places.  
\- Long-term: calendar, memories, dreams, Tehran map, Then & Now.

\#\# Definition of done for any screen  
The screen works in Persian RTL and English LTR, uses semantic tokens only, has empty/loading/error/locked states, works at 360/768/1280px, passes keyboard use and contrast checks, and contains at most one dominant decorative composition.
